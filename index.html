How Double Wordle works

Two five-letter words are hidden at the same time. Enter one guess and it is checked against both boards. You have seven shared guesses to solve both words.

Green means correct letter and position. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in another position. Grey means it is not in that word.

How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

WORDS

Green means the letter is in the word and in the correct spot.

PLANT

Yellow means the letter is in the word, but in a different spot. Grey means it is not in the word.

Use your keyboard or the on-screen keys. You get one puzzle per day.
